# Glossary

Terms the docs and plans use in a project-specific or uncommon sense.

- **Combine chain**: the sectors a position falls back to when it isn't staffed, from the SOP's "combines to" column, such as Licke to Toga to Morgan.
- **CX**: complex, a named group of airports from NCT SOP §1-4. "San Jose CX" includes SJC, NUQ, PAO, RHV and E16.
- **Doubt**: a note the NCT extractor attaches to a draft row or P-ACP line saying what looks wrong in it, such as two altitudes in one cell, for the data review to settle.
- **Entry route / exit route**: a row in an NCT SOP sector table giving how an aircraft enters or leaves that sector: the neighbouring sector, destination or route, aircraft class, altitude and heading or other instructions.
- **Errata list**: `docs/nct-errata.md`, generated by `tools/check_nct_reciprocals.py`: the cells where an exit row and its reciprocal disagree, rows with no reciprocal, and the discrepancies found by hand.
- **FDIO**: in an NCT exit route, an altitude appropriate for direction of flight that keeps the aircraft in the receiving sector's airspace (SOP §1-13).
- **Flow**: the active runway configuration that selects which tables apply, such as SFOW, SFOE, OAKE, SJCE, SMFS, SMFN, RNOS or RNON (SOP §1-6).
- **LOA**: Letter of Agreement, the document that sets procedures between two facilities, such as NCT and ZOA.
- **NCT**: Northern California TRACON.
- **P-ACP**: Pre-Arranged Coordination Procedure, which lets one controller's aircraft enter another's airspace without coordinating each one (SOP §1-18).
- **Reciprocal**: the entry row in the receiving sector's table for a handoff that the sending sector's table lists as an exit row, or the other way round.
- **Reciprocal check**: comparing a sector's exit row with the neighbouring sector's matching entry row to find where the two tables disagree.
- **RV**: in an NCT exit route, the aircraft can be direct or on a vector (SOP §1-13).
- **Specific flow**: a flow that is a subset of another: SFO10 and SFO19 under SFOE, OAKE and SJCE under SFOW. An OAKE or SJCE table replaces the sector's SFOW table; SFO10 and SFO19 replace row by row.
- **ZOA**: Oakland ARTCC, also called Oakland Center.
